`emergency_unwind`. Unit tests: `pause_blocks_new_exposure_but_never_exits_or_repayments`,
`pauser_cannot_unpause_and_stranger_cannot_pause` (vault), `pause_blocks_open_loan_and_new_offers_but_not_repayment_or_accrual`,
`pauser_cannot_unpause_loan_manager` (loan manager).
